01 — Why CareerX exists

Sunday faith and Monday work aren't supposed to live in separate folders. We build the bridge.

Most career advice is either purely secular ambition or purely spiritual platitude. CareerX Network is for the space in between: real decisions about jobs, money, ethics, and calling — worked through with people who share your faith and understand your industry.

02 — What we do

Three ways the network shows up for you

Mentorship pairing

We match you one-on-one with a mentor a few steps ahead in your field — not for networking clout, but for the calls you actually need to make.

Skill & career workshops

Practical sessions on negotiation, career pivots, leadership under pressure, and building a working life you won't need to recover from.

Cross-industry gatherings

Quarterly meetups that mix finance, healthcare, tech, education, and the trades — because your next good idea usually comes from outside your own field.

03 — How members move through it

The path, not a program

There's no single track — but here's how most members' first year looks.

Show up

Come to one gathering. No forms, no commitment — just conversation with people further along a similar road.

Get matched

Tell us your field and your season of work. We introduce you to a mentor and a small peer circle.

Do the work

Monthly check-ins, workshops when they're useful, and a group that will actually ask how the job hunt or the hard boss situation is going.

Turn around and mentor

Most members eventually become the "further along" voice for someone else. That's the network compounding.
