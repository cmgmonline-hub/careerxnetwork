01 — Why CareerX exists 我們為何存在

Sunday faith and Monday work aren't supposed to live in separate folders. We build the bridge. 信仰與週間的工作，不應該活在分開兩個的世界中，我們就是建立起這橋樑。

Most career advice is either purely secular ambition or purely spiritual platitude. CareerX Network is for the space in between: real decisions about jobs, money, ethics, and calling — worked through with people who share your faith and understand your industry. 大部分的職涯建議，不是純粹世俗的野心追求，就是流於表面的屬靈口號。CareerX Network 專注於兩者之間的空間：與同樣持守信仰、又了解你所在行業的人一起，面對關於工作、金錢、倫理與呼召的真實抉擇。

02 — What we do 我們做什麼

Three ways the network shows up for you 三種我們陪伴你的方式

Mentorship pairing 導師配對小組

We place you into small groups, guided and supported by mentors a few steps ahead in your field — not for networking clout, but for the guidance you actually need when it counts. 我們會把你分配到小組，由業界稍為領先幾步的導師提供支持及指引——不是為了建立人脈，而是為了在你真正需要作決定的時刻，給你實質的幫助。

Skill & career workshops 技能與職涯工作坊

Practical sessions on negotiation, career pivots, leadership under pressure, and building a working life you won't need to recover from. 實用工作坊，主題涵蓋談判技巧、轉換跑道、壓力下的領導力，以及建立一個不會讓你身心俱疲的職業生涯。

Cross-industry gatherings 跨行業聚會

Quarterly meetups that mix finance, healthcare, tech, education, and the trades — because your next good idea usually comes from outside your own field. 每季一次的聚會，結合金融、醫療、科技、教育及各行各業的朋友——因為你下一個好點子，往往來自你所在領域以外。

03 — How members move through it 會員的成長歷程

The path, not a program 這是一條路，不是一個課程

There's no single track — but here's how most members' first year looks. 沒有單一固定的路徑——但這是大部分會員第一年大致的樣貌。

Show up 出席

Come to one gathering. No forms, no commitment — just conversation with people further along a similar road. 先來參加一次聚會。不用填表，不需要承諾——只是與走在相似道路、稍為領先的人交流。

Get matched 配對

Tell us your field and your season of work. We introduce you to a mentor and a small peer circle. 告訴我們你的行業與現在的工作階段，我們會為你介紹一位導師，以及一個小型同伴圈子。

Do the work 投入其中

Monthly check-ins, workshops when they're useful, and a group that will actually ask how the job hunt or the hard boss situation is going. 每月的關心跟進、按需要參與的工作坊，以及一個真心關心你求職進度或難搞上司狀況的群體。

Turn around and mentor 轉身成為導師

Most members eventually become the "further along" voice for someone else. That's the network compounding. 大部分會員最終都會成為別人眼中「稍為領先」的那個聲音。這就是網絡的延續與增長。
